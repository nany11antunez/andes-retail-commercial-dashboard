**Andes Retail Group Analysis**

This project presents an interactive commercial performance dashboard developed for Andes Retail Group, a retail company operating in Peru, Chile, and Colombia.

The dashboard analyses sales performance across 2024–2025, exploring revenue, customer segments, product categories, countries, and temporal patterns to provide a clear view of commercial performance.

## 🧠 Analysis Objective

The objective of this project was to transform transactional retail data into an interactive dashboard that provides a clear view of commercial performance and helps identify relevant business patterns.

The analysis focuses on:

- Revenue and gross profit performance.
- Gross margin across markets and product categories.
- Customer segment contribution.
- Differences between Peru, Chile, and Colombia.
- Seasonal revenue patterns.
- Potential opportunities to improve commercial performance.

## 🗂️ Data

The dataset covers 2024–2025 and includes transactional information from Andes Retail Group's operations in:
- Peru
- Chile
- Colombia
  
The business operates across four product categories:

- Electronics
- Clothing
- Sports
- Home

The dashboard was developed in Tableau and includes interactive views for analysing commercial performance from both an overview and detailed perspective.

## 🔎 Analysis Process

The project involved:
* **Data connection and validation.**
* **Preparation of transactional data for analysis.**
* **KPI development for revenue, gross profit, and margin.**
* **Analysis by country, product category, and customer segment.**
* **Monthly and seasonal analysis.**
* **Interactive dashboard development.**
* **Business storytelling using the SQCA (Situation, Complication, Question, Answer) framework.**

## 📊 Key Findings

- Andes Retail Group generated approximately $5.53M in total revenue, with $1.94M in gross profit and a gross margin of approximately 35.1% across 2024–2025.
- The analysis identified a strong seasonal pattern, with revenue declining from February and reaching its lowest levels during the middle of the year.
- Revenue patterns across 2024 and 2025 showed similar trajectories, indicating limited change in the overall seasonal pattern between the two years.
- Gross margin remained relatively stable, ranging from approximately 34.67% to 35.62% across the analysed regions and product categories.
- Colombia recorded approximately $1.35M in revenue and $470K in gross profit, while Peru and Chile recorded higher gross profit levels within the analysed sample.
- Premium customers represented 44.28% of the customer base in Colombia, compared with 47.72% in Peru and 47.95% in Chile.

## 💡 Business Insights

The dashboard highlights two areas for further commercial investigation:
- Seasonal performance: The pronounced June–August decline suggests an opportunity to investigate mid-year campaigns, cross-selling, and other strategies aimed at maintaining sales activity during the lower-revenue period.
- Customer segmentation in Colombia: The lower proportion of Premium customers provides an opportunity to investigate strategies for increasing Premium customer participation while monitoring the effect on gross margin.
The dashboard allows these patterns to be explored interactively across countries, categories, customer segments, and time periods.
